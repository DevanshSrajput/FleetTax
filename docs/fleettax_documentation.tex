\section{Product Overview}

FleetTax is an offline-first Android application built with Flutter. It is designed for bus and truck fleet owners who need a reliable way to manage road tax deadlines across multiple vehicles. The app stores data locally, calculates tax validity from the last paid date and selected period, highlights urgent vehicles, and supports direct handoff to the Vahan portal for payment.

\subsection{Primary Users}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Fleet owners managing buses, trucks, or mixed commercial fleets.
  \item Office staff responsible for tax renewal tracking.
  \item Operators who need a simple device-local record instead of a spreadsheet or cloud system.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Core Outcomes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Reduce missed tax renewal dates.
  \item Keep vehicle status visible at a glance.
  \item Store payment notes and receipt references with each vehicle.
  \item Continue working even when internet is unavailable.
\end{itemize}

\section{Feature Set}

\subsection{Vehicle Management}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Add, edit, and delete vehicle records.
  \item Store registration number, vehicle type, tax period, last paid date, notes, and receipt reference.
  \item Search vehicles by registration number.
  \item Filter by vehicle type or tax status.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Tax Tracking}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Monthly, quarterly, and yearly tax validity periods.
  \item Automatic expiry calculation from the last payment date.
  \item Three operational states: expired, due soon, and valid.
  \item Dashboard statistics for quick daily review.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Payment Workflow}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Review vehicles marked expired or due soon.
  \item Open the Vahan portal from the app.
  \item Complete the official payment flow.
  \item Mark the vehicle as paid in FleetTax.
  \item Save the new period, payment date, and receipt reference.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Responsibility Map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Vehicle model:} Owns tax period, expiry date, days-left calculation, and status logic.
  \item \textbf{Database helper:} Encapsulates SQLite database creation and CRUD operations.
  \item \textbf{Vehicle provider:} Loads records, applies filters/search, and notifies UI listeners.
  \item \textbf{Notification service:} Initializes local notification channels and dispatches alerts.
  \item \textbf{WorkManager service:} Schedules periodic background checks.
  \item \textbf{Screens and widgets:} Present dashboard, forms, payment flow, and reusable UI components.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Status Rules}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Expired:} Expiry date is before the current date.
  \item \textbf{Due soon:} Expiry date is today or within the next 10 days.
  \item \textbf{Valid:} More than 10 days remain.
\end{itemize}

\section{Notification Flow}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item WorkManager starts the periodic background task.
  \item The task loads all vehicles from SQLite.
  \item Each vehicle computes expiry date and remaining days.
  \item Expired and due-soon vehicles are passed to the notification service.
  \item The notification displays the registration number and urgency.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Troubleshooting}

\subsection{Notifications Do Not Appear}
Check Android notification permission, app battery restrictions, WorkManager registration, and local notification channel initialization.

\subsection{Database Data Looks Stale}
Confirm provider reload calls after insert/update/delete operations. During development, uninstalling the app clears the local SQLite database.

\subsection{Vahan Portal Does Not Open}
Verify that the device has a browser installed and that url\_launcher is configured correctly for Android.
